# Better Is Not the Same as Safe · Rompe el ciclo

A survivor-informed participatory reflection with migrant and refugee girls in and after protective care in Trinidad and Tobago.
Prepared for the **Youth and Survivor Summit, Manila, Philippines, 2026**.

🌐 **Read the report:** https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/rompe-el-ciclo/

## About

This reflection listened equally to two groups of migrant and refugee girls connected to a protective care centre in Trinidad and Tobago: six girls still living there (focus group) and eighteen who have left, most now back in Venezuela (anonymous survey). Both groups used the same lists, so their answers can be compared.

The findings are framed as a water cycle: **evaporation** (why girls leave), **condensation** (exploitation and care), **precipitation** (after leaving) and **the sea** (home, or wherever they land). Support at every stage can break the cycle.

## Main findings

1. Not knowing what will happen and too little family contact hurt most, in both groups.
2. Healing has to be for every girl, not only those judged most affected.
3. Education is their hope for a legal, independent future.
4. Freedom also means how girls live inside care.
5. Better is not the same as safe: risks continue after leaving.
6. The cycle starts with not knowing: girls left without information about trafficking or migration risks.
